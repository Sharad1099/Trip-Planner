Packing list

  • T-shirts for the Tbilisi days
  • 2 to 3 full-sleeve tops
  • Thermal inner set each, for Kazbegi
  • Fleece or hoodie
  • Warm puffer jacket
  • Rain jacket or poncho
  • Comfy trousers or jeans

Feet, head, hands

  • Walking shoes with good grip, waterproof if possible
  • Wool socks plus a spare pair for 2 Oct
  • Flip-flops for the hotel
